# Privacy and Retention Policy (Employee Pulse MVP)

## Purpose
This system supports production operations by identifying work pressure, clarity gaps, resource blockers, and collaboration friction.

It is not a medical, psychological, or personality assessment tool.

---

## What data is collected
1. Weekly email replies to 3 work check-in questions.
2. Structured analysis outputs:
   - workload_score
   - clarity_score
   - collaboration_friction_score
   - resource_gap_score
   - risk_level
   - short summary
   - manager suggestions

---

## What this system must NOT do
- Must not infer mental illness, personality defects, or private-life causes.
- Must not generate automatic punishment or HR disciplinary actions.
- Must not be used as a single source for employment decisions.

---

## Data retention rules
### Temporary data
- Raw email reply text is temporary.
- Raw text is deleted or excluded after monthly summaries are finalized.

### Long-term data
- Keep only structured scores, short work summaries, and management suggestions.
- Keep trend-level monthly outputs for planning and support.

---

## Access control
- Limit access to operations manager and approved leadership only.
- Use dedicated workspace accounts where possible.
- Do not forward raw replies broadly.

---

## Human review requirement
- All high-risk outputs require manager review and contextual verification.
- The system output is a support signal, not a verdict.

---

## Employee transparency statement (recommended)
Share this clearly with participants:
- The purpose is workflow support and blocker reduction.
- The system focuses on work conditions, not private life.
- Raw reply text is temporary.
- Summaries are used to improve planning, resources, and coordination.

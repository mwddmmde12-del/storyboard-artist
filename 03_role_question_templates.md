# Role-Based Weekly Question Templates

Use these as the **default weekly questions**.  
Keep the wording stable for trend consistency.

---

## 1) Fad / Fadiran Olumide (QC + First Review)

1. This week, how manageable was your first-review queue volume?
2. Where did quality/compliance checks slow you down most (AI misuse, labels, specs, brand safety, etc.)?
3. What one support change would reduce rework or review bottlenecks next week?

---

## 2) Chiemeli Kanikwu (Performance Bridge)

1. How clear were scripts for actor execution this week?
2. Which scenes were hardest to make believable or performance-ready?
3. What one pre-shoot clarification would improve performance quality next week?

---

## 3) Chinonyerem Emmanuella (Set Operations)

1. How ready were actors, props, and locations before shoot this week?
2. What local blockers disrupted execution most (timing, logistics, weather, power, network, etc.)?
3. What one planning change would make next week’s shoots smoother?

---

## Operator notes
- Ask only work-related questions.
- Avoid private-life or psychological language.
- Keep each weekly email short and clear.
